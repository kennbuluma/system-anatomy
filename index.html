System Anatomy

System anatomy is a visual representation of the key components and interactions within a system. It helps to understand the structure and behavior of a system by illustrating how different parts are connected and how they work together.

In a system anatomy diagram, you typically see various components such as modules, subsystems, or services, along with their relationships and dependencies. This can include data flow, control flow, and communication between components.

System anatomy diagrams are often used in software engineering, systems engineering, and other fields to facilitate communication among team members, identify potential issues, and guide the design and development process.
