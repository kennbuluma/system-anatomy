PRIVACY POLICY

How we protect, handle, and respect your data in our architectural analysis and system optimization services.

Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide our system architecture analysis and optimization services:

  • System performance metrics and logs (anonymized)
  • Architecture diagrams and technical specifications
  • Contact information for project communication
  • Usage data from our web platform and tools

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Delivering architectural analysis and recommendations
  • Improving our services and methodologies
  • Communicating about your projects and system health
  • Ensuring compliance with security and performance standards

Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures:

  • End-to-end encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure cloud infrastructure with SOC 2 compliance
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Access controls and audit logging
  • Zero-trust architecture principles

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary:

  • Project data: Retained for 7 years for compliance and analysis
  • Communication records: Retained for 3 years
  • System logs: Anonymized and retained for 1 year
  • You can request data deletion at any time

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request data deletion
  • Object to data processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time
